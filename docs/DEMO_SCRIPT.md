# EnglAI 1.0 Demo — 5–7 Minutes

1. Open the futuristic Public Page and show Teacher Login, Student Login, Guest Join, and Public Demo.
2. Log in as Teacher, create/open a Classroom, upload its RPP, run analysis, confirm level, and generate content.
3. Open a Student account/Guest browser, join with Classroom ID, and play one Self Learning activity.
4. Create a Mixed Skills or Final Challenge Live Quiz, join from two browsers, start, answer, and show EVALUATING plus the final podium.
5. Open Analytics, show real skill/competency metrics and a generated recommendation.
6. Review a Speaking/Writing assessment, apply a reasoned override, and show immutable audit history.
7. Open Settings, reports, readiness health, and the EnglAI 1.0 release version.
