# Student Guide

- Register a Student account to continue progress across devices, or use clearly labeled Guest access for a demo.
- Join with a valid Classroom ID. A Classroom may require Teacher approval.
- Complete Reading, Listening, Speaking, and Writing Self Learning activities.
- Browser Speech Synthesis is labeled Generated Listening Audio.
- Speaking uses transcription-based AI Feedback and does not claim phoneme, accent, stress, intonation, or pronunciation accuracy.
- Join Live Quiz with a display name and avatar, then wait for the Teacher to start.
- Progress contains personal activity and quiz history only.
